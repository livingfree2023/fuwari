Heavenly Father, as I open your Holy Word,

I ask for the light of the Holy Spirit to guide me.

Open the eyes of my heart and mind,

so that I may truly understand the truths You have for me today.

Let Your Word be a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.

Help me not only to read Your Word but to live it out in my life.

In Jesus' Name I pray, Amen.

* * *

天父，当我翻开您的圣言时，

我祈求圣灵的光来指引我。 求您开启我心眼和心智，

使我今天能真正明白您要赐给我的真理。 

愿您的圣言成为我脚前的灯我路上的光。 

帮助我不仅阅读您的圣言，更能将它活在我的生命中。 

我奉耶稣的名祷告，阿门。
